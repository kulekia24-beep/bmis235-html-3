Messi

Operating primarily from the right flank with complete freedom to roam centrally, Messi combined elite dribbling, unmatched vision, and lethal left-footed finishing. During the MSN era, he evolved from a pure "false nine" goalscorer into the ultimate hybrid of deep-lying creator and clinical finisher.

Messi served as the offensive brain and primary distributor of the team. He regularly dropped into the midfield to receive the ball, drawing defenders toward him and breaking defensive lines with long diagonal passes to Neymar or threaded through-balls to Suárez. Once the ball progressed into the final third, he drifted back toward the penalty box to finish plays.
