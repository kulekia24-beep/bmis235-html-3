Neymar

Positioned on the left wing, Neymar brought explosive pace, Brazilian flair, close-control dribbling, and high-level playmaking. He was fearless in taking on defenders and consistently stepped up in high-pressure knockout matches.

Neymar’s primary job was to provide width and stretch opposing defenses horizontally. By hugging the left touchline, he forced right-backs to stay wide, preventing defenses from crowding the middle where Messi operated. When isolated one-on-one, Neymar used his speed and trickery to beat his marker, either driving to the byline to square the ball to Suárez or cutting inside onto his stronger right foot to shoot or combine with Messi.
