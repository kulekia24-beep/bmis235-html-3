Suarez

Suarez was a relentless, physical, and fiercely competitive number nine known for his unpredictability, world-class movement, and ability to score from difficult angles. His arrival in 2014 gave Barcelona a true center forward for the first time in years.

Suarez acted as the focal point and chaos creator in the opponent's penalty area. He constantly pinned center-backs back, made selfless decoy runs to drag defenders out of position, and created open pockets of space for Messi and Neymar to cut inside. In front of goal, he was ruthless, converting half-chances and thriving on quick one-touch combinations in tight spaces.
