MSN

From 2014 to 2017, Lionel Messi, Luis Suárez, and Neymar Jr. united as "MSN" to form the most lethal and unselfish forward line in FC Barcelona history.

MSN is the famous attacking trio of Lionel Messi, Luis Suárez, and Neymar Jr. that played for FC Barcelona from 2014 to 2017. Widely regarded as one of the greatest forward lines in soccer history, the trio combined individual brilliance with rare unselfishness, chemistry, and close friendship off the pitch.
